Frequently Asked Questions

What is the shelf life of your moringa powder?

Our moringa powder has a shelf life of 24 months when stored properly in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. We recommend keeping it sealed to maintain freshness.

How should I take moringa powder?

Start with 1 teaspoon per day and gradually increase to 1-2 tablespoons. You can mix it into smoothies, juice, tea, or sprinkle it over food. It's best taken with food.

Is your moringa powder organic?

Yes! All our moringa powder is certified USDA organic, non-GMO, and free from pesticides, additives, and preservatives.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we ship within the United States. We're working on expanding our international shipping options. Please check back soon!

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, contact us for a full refund or replacement.

Are there any side effects?

Moringa is generally safe for most people. However, if you're pregnant, nursing, or taking medications, please consult your healthcare provider before use.
